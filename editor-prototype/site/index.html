The Living Folio

A beautifully typeset book, still being written. Set in Lora and IBM Plex Sans, on warm paper with dark ink and a blue second pen.

Essays, notes, and sketches each have their own sequence of folio numbers. Side notes sit beside the passages they belong to, and dated additions show how a page has changed.

Built for the screen, with room for the unfinished.
